---
title: Use the palette
description: "Search everything from one box, pin what you open most, and set reminders and to-dos from the palette."
---

Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd>. One search covers the sessions of every project, GitHub, runs, files and symbols, views, commands, settings and memory.

## Narrow the search

| Start with | To search |
| --- | --- |
| `>` | commands |
| `/` | files |
| `:` | settings |
| `@` | people |
| `#` | pull requests |

`#` lists every open pull request of your projects' repositories, not only yours. `#86` finds that one even when it is closed or merged. A first word that names a repository or project narrows to it, as in `#arakun 86`.

## Move through rows

- <kbd>→</kbd> opens a row's actions or goes into it, such as a project, a person or Branches. <kbd>←</kbd> goes back.
- <kbd>Space</kbd>, after the arrows, marks rows for the bar at the bottom.
- <kbd>Ctrl+Enter</kbd> asks an agent.

## Pin what you open most

<kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd> on a row pins it to the top of the palette and the sidebar.

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Ctrl+1…9</kbd> | Open a pin, in the palette |
| <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+1…9</kbd> | Open a pin, anywhere |
| <kbd>Alt+Up</kbd>, <kbd>Alt+Down</kbd> | Reorder pins |

## Reminders and to-dos

Type a sentence:

- `remind me in 20 min to review the PR`
- `remind me at 5pm to stretch`
- `timer 10 min`

A reminder rings on the [Dynamic Island](/dynamic-island). One that comes due while Arakun is closed rings the next time it opens, marked as missed.

`to-do: …`, or a reminder with no time, adds a to-do. Type `to-do` to list them, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on one to tick it off.

## Ask in plain words

- A question offers a short answer from Claude Haiku, through Claude Code. Nothing is sent until you press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the offer.
- A sentence such as `merge #64 when the checks pass and tell me` becomes an automation once you confirm it. If Arakun can't read it, it offers to have Claude read it, again only on <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Open it from any app

```sh
arakun palette
```

This brings the window up with the palette open. Bind it to a global key in your desktop. On Hyprland, for example:

```ini
bind = SUPER, K, exec, arakun palette
```

<Note>
Pins are kept in `pins.json`, reminders in `rules.json`, to-dos in `todos.json` and what you open in `palette-frecency.json`, next to the state file.
</Note>
