---
title: Phone remote
description: Follow your Arakun sessions from a phone, send prompts and answer the agent, over your local network, Tailscale or an encrypted relay you sign in to.
---

Arakun serves a web page that lets a phone follow sessions, read them live, send prompts, stop a turn and answer the agent.

Turn on **Phone** under Arakun › Extensions, or Settings › General › Sidebar. It is already on if you chose to pair a phone in the first-run setup. Then pick how the phone reaches this computer in the Phone tab.

## How the phone reaches your computer

- **Over the local network.** This is the default. You pair the phone with a code.
- **Over Tailscale.** You pair the phone with a code.
- **Through an end-to-end encrypted relay.** You sign in with GitHub on the computer and on the phone. There is no code.

On the local network and over Tailscale, the page comes from the computer itself. It only opens while the computer is on and awake. The relay works from anywhere and says when the computer is offline; the Phone tab then offers **Use Relay**.

## Reach it through the relay

The relay lets a device in only when it is signed in to the same GitHub account as the computer.

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign in on the computer">

With Relay chosen, the Phone tab says "Sign in to reach this computer from anywhere". Press **Sign in with GitHub**. It is the same sign-in as the User row of the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step title="Open the page on the phone">

The Phone tab shows the address to open on the phone, `https://app.arakun.dev`, with its QR code.

</Step>
<Step title="Sign in on the phone and pick the computer">

Sign in with GitHub as the same account, then pick the computer. With only one computer, the page opens it straight away.

</Step>
</Steps>

The first time a device connects, a notice names it and says it connected for the first time.

Signed in, the Phone tab lists:

- **Signed-in devices:** every device signed in to the account, with its kind and when it was last used. **Sign out** signs one out.
- **Your computers:** the account's computers that run the app, online or when last seen. **Remove** takes one off the account, after a question that names it. A computer still signed in comes back the next time it connects.

On the phone, each computer in the list has **Remove**, and the page's Phone tab lists the account's signed-in devices with **Sign out**. With several computers, push notifications come only from the computer you opened last.

<Warning>
Anyone who signs in to your GitHub account, or holds one of its sessions, can reach every computer of the account. Sign out the devices you do not know.
</Warning>

<Note>
A phone paired through the relay with a link before v0.19.0 must sign in again. Pairing over the local network or Tailscale is unchanged.
</Note>

## Pair a phone on the network

Over the local network or Tailscale, press **Pair a phone**. Scan the code with the phone's camera. Or open the page on the phone and type the code, or send it the link.

The code or link works once. Each phone gets a key of its own, which you can revoke under **Paired phones**. That list shows only the phones paired with a code.

## Run your own relay

The relay defaults to the hosted one at `app.arakun.dev`. The relay can't hand your computer's address to anyone else: it comes from a key only your computer has.

You can run your own: it is a Cloudflare Worker in the `cloud/` folder of the repository. Point `relay_base` in `phone.json` at it, and set `ARAKUN_TEAMS_URL` to the same server. Arakun sends your session only to the server that issued it, so when the two differ the relay stays signed out and the Phone tab names both addresses.

## Send prompts

While the agent works, a prompt you send waits for the turn to end. With something written, **Stop** becomes **Stop and send**.

## Shortcuts

The page keeps shortcuts, phrases you send often, as a row of buttons over the box.

- A shortcut that sends right away shows while the agent waits for you. The others show while you write, and fill the box.
- `{number}` in a shortcut is a blank you type each time, on the number keyboard.
- Hold a message you sent to keep it as a shortcut, or tap **+** in the row.

Shortcuts stay in that phone's browser.

## Charts and diagrams

Charts and diagrams in a reply (` ```chart ` and ` ```mermaid ` blocks) are drawn on the phone as they are in the app. **Code** shows the block's text, and **Open** shows the drawing full screen.

## Servers

A phone connected to the app also sees and works in the sessions of your [servers](/server).

<Tip>
In redacted mode, the Phone tab hides the pairing code, its addresses and the names of paired phones. **Copy link** still copies the real link.
</Tip>
