The Dynamic Island is a small overlay at the top of the screen. It shows what the agents of every project are doing: a dot per busy session, and a card when one finishes, fails or needs an answer.

## Where it works

<Callout type="warn">
  Linux on Wayland only, for now. It needs a compositor with the wlr-layer-shell protocol. Windows has no Dynamic Island yet.
</Callout>

It runs as its own process, `arakun island-host`.
