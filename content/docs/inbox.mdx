---
title: Inbox
description: Every notice, grouped by day.
---

Every notice Arakun shows, on the island or as a desktop notification, also lands in the sidebar's Inbox. It sits under the sessions waiting on you, grouped by day.

- Click a notice to open what it is about.
- The badge counts the unread ones. They count as read when you close the Inbox.
- Arakun keeps the last 300.

## From a terminal

```sh
arakun notices --unseen
```
