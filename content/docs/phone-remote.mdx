---
title: Phone remote
description: Follow sessions and answer the agent from a phone.
---

Arakun serves a web page that lets a paired phone follow sessions, send prompts and answer the agent.

## How the phone reaches your computer

- Over the local network. This is the default.
- Over Tailscale.
- Through an end-to-end encrypted relay.

## The relay

The relay defaults to the hosted one at `arakun.dev`. You can run your own: it is a Cloudflare Worker in the `relay/` folder of the repository. Point `relay_base` in `phone.json` at it.
